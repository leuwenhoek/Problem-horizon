Hackathon · Problem Statement

Use AI to make learning easy, guided, and simple.

The brief

Build an AI-powered learning experience that makes acquiring a new skill feel easy instead of overwhelming. It should meet a learner wherever they are, generate a clear roadmap of what to learn next, and guide them step by step — so progress feels obvious and no one gets lost along the way.

What you’ll build

  • 01A working prototype where a learner picks a goal and gets an AI-generated roadmap.
  • 02A guided path: clear steps, next actions, and a visible sense of progress.
  • 03A 60-second demo taking one skill from “where do I start?” to a confident first step.

Extra points — judged only on these two

+ POINTS

Gamification

Progress, streaks, feedback, reward. Does learning feel motivating enough to keep people coming back for the next step?

+ POINTS

Simplicity

Zero friction, nothing to figure out. Can any learner understand the path in one glance and take the next step with no confusion?

Make skills easy to reach. Win on the two things that matter.
